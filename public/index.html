        How to play

        • On your turn, put tiles in one row or column so they read as a word and touch tiles already on the board. The first word must cover the centre star.
        • Tap a tile on your rack, then tap a square. Or drag it there. Tap a tile you just placed to take it back.
        • With the keyboard: click a square, type letters, and they're placed left to right. Click the same square again, or press Space, to type top to bottom instead. Backspace takes the last one back.
        • If a word isn't in the dictionary, or your tiles break a placement rule, the tiles turn red and the line under your rack says why.
        • Every new word you make counts, including ones formed across. Using all 7 tiles in one go earns 50 bonus points.
        • Swap trades tiles with the bag and uses your turn. Pass skips your turn.
        • The game ends when someone uses their last tile and the bag is empty, or after six turns in a row with no points. Leftover tiles count against you.
